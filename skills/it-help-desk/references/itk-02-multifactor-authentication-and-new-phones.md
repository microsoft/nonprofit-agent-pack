# Multifactor Authentication (MFA) and New Phones

> Generic Microsoft 365 help for the Nonprofit Agent Pack IT Help Desk. Some steps depend on settings your IT team controls. Replace or extend these articles with your own IT procedures.

## Set up or change your sign-in methods

1. From a computer, go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo.
2. Select Add sign-in method and add Microsoft Authenticator on the new phone.
3. Remove the old phone only after the new one works.

Keep your old phone until you've signed in and re-registered your work account on the new one. Authenticator backups don't move work or school accounts, and iPhone and Android backups can't be swapped.

Choose the Authenticator app when you can. Text and voice codes depend on your organization's setup.

## Lost phone

- Contact your IT team. If your organization uses Temporary Access Pass, IT can verify your identity and give you a short-lived pass to set up the new phone.

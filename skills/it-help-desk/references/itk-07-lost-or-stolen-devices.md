# Lost or Stolen Laptops and Phones

> Generic Microsoft 365 help for the Nonprofit Agent Pack IT Help Desk. Some steps depend on settings your IT team controls. Replace or extend these articles with your own IT procedures.

1. Contact IT right away so they can revoke your sessions and, if the device is managed, lock or wipe it. A wipe erases everything on the device.
2. From another device, change your password at https://myaccount.microsoft.com (Change password). If you can't sign in, reset it at https://aka.ms/sspr.
3. If the device may contain client or donor information, tell your privacy contact the same day.

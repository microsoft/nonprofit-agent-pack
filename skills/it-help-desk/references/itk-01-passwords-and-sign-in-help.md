# Passwords and Sign-in Help

> Generic Microsoft 365 help for the Nonprofit Agent Pack IT Help Desk. Some steps depend on settings your IT team controls. Replace or extend these articles with your own IT procedures.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Go to https://aka.ms/sspr and enter your work email address.
2. Verify your identity with a method you registered. The Microsoft Authenticator app works best. Text and voice codes depend on your organization's setup.
3. Choose a new password.

If you see a message to contact your administrator, self-service reset isn't turned on for you, so contact IT.

## Account locked

- Wait a minute or two before trying again. More failed attempts make the lockout longer.
- Or reset your password at https://aka.ms/sspr. If asked, choose I forgot my password. A reset ends the lockout.

## Good password habits

- Use a long passphrase.
- Never reuse your work password anywhere else.
- Never share your password or a sign-in code with anyone. IT will never ask for them.

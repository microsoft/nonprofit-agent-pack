# Microsoft Teams Quick Fixes

> Generic Microsoft 365 help for the Nonprofit Agent Pack IT Help Desk. Some steps depend on settings your IT team controls. Replace or extend these articles with your own IT procedures.

- No audio or camera: in a meeting, select the arrow next to Mic or Camera to choose your devices. Outside a meeting, go to Settings and more (...) > Settings > Devices. Close other apps that use the camera, and check your computer's privacy settings.
- Teams running slowly: quit Teams fully (right-click the Teams icon on the taskbar > Quit) and reopen it. Clear the cache only if IT asks: in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps, select More options (...) next to Microsoft Teams, then Advanced options > Reset. This removes your Teams preferences.
- Can't find a chat or file: use the search bar at the top. Files shared in a channel or chat are on its Shared tab.
- Guests: guests can usually join from the meeting link without a Teams account, unless your organization blocks it.

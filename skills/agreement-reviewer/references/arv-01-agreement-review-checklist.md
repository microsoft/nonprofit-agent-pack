# Grant and Contract Agreement Review Checklist

> General guidance for US nonprofits as of October 2026, not legal, tax, or accounting advice. Rules elsewhere differ. Review with qualified advisors before relying on it.

Review every agreement against these clause categories. For each, state what the agreement says (quote and cite the section), the obligation it creates, and a risk rating. The risk signals are this tool's review heuristics, not legal limits.

| Category | What to look for | Risk signals (review heuristics, not legal limits) |
|---|---|---|
| Parties, term, and amount | Legal names, start and end dates, total award, payment schedule | Start date before signature; payments tied to undefined milestones |
| Scope and deliverables | What must be delivered and by when | Deliverables broader than the proposal; 'and other duties as requested' |
| Reporting | Report types, due dates, required measures, format | Reports due in 15 days or less (federal defaults are 30 to 120 days); new measures not in the proposal |
| Budget changes | Rebudgeting authority and approval thresholds | Any line change requires prior written approval |
| Allowable costs and indirect | Allowable and unallowable costs, indirect rate cap | Indirect cap lower than the proposal assumed |
| Match and cost share | Amount, type, documentation | Match must be cash only or documented monthly |
| Payment and repayment | Advance or reimbursement, unspent funds, clawback | Repayment of funds for missing a single target |
| Records, audit, retention | Retention period, audit rights, access | Retention longer than 7 years or beyond the funder's rule (federal baseline is 3 years after the final financial report, 2 CFR 200.334); funder may audit with no notice |
| Intellectual property | Ownership and license of materials created | Assignment of all IP to the funder |
| Publicity and acknowledgment | Logo use, approval of press | Funder approval required for all communications |
| Data privacy and security | Participant data sharing, breach notice | Sharing identifiable participant data with the funder; breach notice under 72 hours |
| Insurance and indemnification | Coverage levels, who indemnifies whom | One-way, uncapped indemnification by the nonprofit |
| Termination | For cause, for convenience, notice periods | Funder may terminate for convenience with no payment for work performed |
| Federal flow-down | References to 2 CFR 200 (Uniform Guidance), an Assistance Listing number (formerly CFDA), subaward language | Federal pass-through funds usually carry Uniform Guidance procurement, cost-principle, and reporting rules, and count toward the Single Audit threshold when received as a subrecipient (not a contractor) |
| Governing law and disputes | State law, venue, arbitration | Venue in a distant state; mandatory arbitration at the nonprofit's cost |

## Risk rating

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Could cost money, trigger repayment, or expose the organization to liability; negotiate before signing |
| Medium | Creates real operating burden or ambiguity; clarify in writing |
| Low | Standard language; note the obligation and move on |

## Federal funds note

If the agreement passes through federal money, flag it. Under 2 CFR 200.501 (2024 revision, effective October 1, 2024), a non-federal entity that expends $1,000,000 or more in federal awards in its fiscal year generally needs a single audit (or, in limited cases, a program-specific audit). Payments received as a contractor don't count. OMB proposed further changes to 2 CFR 200 in May 2026 (not final as of October 2026). Confirm the first applicable fiscal year with your auditor.

## Always include

- A plain-language summary for the executive director and board (5 bullets maximum).
- Questions to send the funder before signing.
- A reminder that this is not legal advice and that counsel should review High-risk items.

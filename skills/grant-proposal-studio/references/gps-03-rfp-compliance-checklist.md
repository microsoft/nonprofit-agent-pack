# RFP Compliance Checklist

> General guidance for US nonprofits as of October 2026, not legal, tax, or accounting advice. Rules elsewhere differ. Review with qualified advisors before relying on it.

Extract every one of these items from a funder's RFP, guidelines, or call for proposals and build a compliance matrix. Quote the RFP and give its page or section number. Mark anything not stated as 'Not specified' rather than guessing.

| Category | Items to extract |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Organization type, geography, population, budget size, prior-grantee rules |
| Deadlines | Letter of inquiry, full proposal, questions period, decision date, project start and end |
| Funding | Award range, number of awards, match requirement, indirect cost cap, multi-year rules |
| Format | Page or word limits per section, font and size, margins, file type, naming convention, portal |
| Required sections | Every narrative section and the order required |
| Attachments | Budget form, IRS determination letter or affirmation letter (US; fiscally sponsored projects may use the sponsor's), audit or financials, board list, logic model, letters of support |
| Scoring | Published review criteria and their weights |
| Reporting | Interim and final report timing, required measures, site visits |
| Restrictions | Unallowable costs, lobbying, capital, endowment, religious activity, subgranting |

## Compliance matrix format

Columns: Requirement | RFP reference | Where we address it | Status (Ready, Draft, Missing, Not specified) | Owner | Notes.
